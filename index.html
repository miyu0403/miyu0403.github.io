About Me

Hi 👋, I am a master’s student in Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University, advised by Zhixuan Chu. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree in Cyberspace Security from Shandong University.

My research focuses on Trustworthy (M)LLMs, including hallucination detection and mitigation, multimodal retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic reinforcement learning.

I am open to research collaborations. Feel free to get in touch!
